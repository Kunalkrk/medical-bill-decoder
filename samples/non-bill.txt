Simple Tomato Soup

Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- Salt and pepper to taste

Directions:
1. Heat the oil in a pot and cook the onion until soft, about 5 minutes.
2. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
3. Add tomatoes and broth; simmer 20 minutes.
4. Blend until smooth and season to taste.
